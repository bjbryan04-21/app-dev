# PEE MAK (2013)

## A. Introduction

**Pee Mak** is one of the Thai horror-comedy movie released on March 28, 2013. It was directed by **Banjong Pisanthanakun** and stars **Mario Maurer** and **Davika Hoorne**. The movie it combines the comedy, horror, romance, and friendship in one entertaining story. It is one of my favorite thai - movies because it makes me laugh, feel scared, and feel emotional at the same time.

## B. Synopsis

The story it takes place during the early Rattanakosin period in the Thailand. Mak is a soldier who has to leave his pregnant wife named Nak, to fight in a war. During the war, he meets the four soldiers who become his closest friends in the war: **Ter**, **Puak**, **Shin**, and **Aey**. After the war, Mak it invites his friends to visit his home and meet his wife Nak and his newborn child. However, his friends is discovered a terrifying secret: Nak and her baby may already be dead before Mak came home. They try to tell to Mak the truth, but he refuses to believe them because of his deep love for his wife Nak.

## C. Characters

Here are the the five important characters in the movie Pee-Mak:

1. **Mak** – Played by Mario Maurer. He is the loving husband who deeply loves his wife, Nak.
2. **Nak** – Played by Davika Hoorne. She is Mak's beautiful and devoted wife who has a mysterious secret.
3. **Ter** – Played by Nattapong Chartpong. He is one of the Mak's four soldier friends in the war and often helps the group.
4. **Puak** – Played by Pongsatorn Jongwilas. He is one of Mak's funny and loyal friends.
5. **Shin** – Played by Wiwat Kongrasri. He is one of Mak's friends who becomes suspicious about Mak's wife, Nak.

6. **Aey** – Played by Kantapat Permpoonpatcharasuk. He is another loyal friend who helps to uncover the truth about his wife.
